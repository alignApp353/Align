Pricing for employers

Start with a free slot. Add capacity when you need it.

Alygnn sells reusable job slots — not individual job ads. Keep a job in a slot, close or delete it when you are done, then use that available slot for your next opening.

Choose how long you want your hiring capacity.

Quarterly, monthly, and weekly options use active reusable job slots. Job Boost is available on every plan for $15 per day.

Your first job slot is free

1 active reusable job slot with access to up to 25 candidates total. No credit card required. After the 25-candidate limit is reached, Alygnn can offer a $15/day Job Boost or a $99 weekly job slot.

The free job slot is a one-time employer offer and includes access to up to 25 candidates total. Weekly job slots begin immediately upon successful purchase and expire 7 days after purchase. Monthly and quarterly plans count active reusable job slots — when a role is closed or deleted, that slot becomes available for another job while the paid plan remains active. The $150/month single-slot option is a standalone reusable paid job slot and is limited to one active single-slot subscription per employer. Job Boost is an optional $15-per-day add-on for the job currently occupying an active slot and does not extend the slot term.
