Pricing for employers

Start with a free slot. Add capacity when you need it.

Alygnn sells reusable job slots — not individual job ads. Keep a job in a slot, close or delete it when you are done, then use that available slot for your next opening.

Choose how long you want your hiring capacity.

Your permanent free slot is always included. Paid plans add reusable capacity on top of it.

Your first job slot is free

1 active reusable job slot with access to up to 25 candidates total. No credit card required. After the 25-candidate limit is reached, add another job slot when you need more hiring capacity.
