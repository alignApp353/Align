AI & matching

Alygnn may use AI to support matching, resume interpretation, job-writing assistance, and content review. AI output can be imperfect and is intended to assist—not replace—human judgment.

1
Recommendations are not decisions.

Matching tools help surface potentially relevant opportunities or candidates. Employers remain responsible for employment decisions.

2
Users should review AI-assisted content.

Draft job descriptions and other generated content should be checked for accuracy, relevance, and legal compliance before use.

3
Employment laws still apply.

Employers remain responsible for using hiring tools in a lawful, non-discriminatory manner and for applying their own independent judgment.

Trust & safety

Alygnn uses employer verification and platform rules to reduce fraudulent activity, but no verification process can eliminate every risk.

1
Employers must provide accurate business information.

Alygnn may request verification details or documents and may approve, delay, decline, suspend, or remove accounts or listings that raise trust or policy concerns.

2
Fake or misleading jobs are prohibited.

Users may not submit fabricated business information, deceptive postings, unlawful content, or other material that violates Alygnn's Terms.

3
Job seekers should protect sensitive information.

Be cautious of requests for money, financial credentials, or unnecessary sensitive information during the recruiting process, and independently verify suspicious offers.

Important: Employer verification is a risk-reduction measure, not a guarantee of an employer's identity, conduct, financial condition, or the legitimacy of every job opportunity.
